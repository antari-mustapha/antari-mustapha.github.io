About Mustapha Antari
Mustapha Antari is a celebrated Moroccan percussionist and solo recording artist widely recognized for his mastery of the darbuka (oriental goblet drum) and his work in global rhythm fusion.

Key Highlights & Music Career
Master of Oriental Percussion:

Antari is known for his technical agility, precise solo phrasing, and ability to blend traditional Arab-Moroccan drumming patterns with modern global grooves.

Album & Projects (Sur les traces d'Ibn Batouta):

One of his signature creative projects is the musical concept "Sur les traces d'Ibn Batouta" (In the Footsteps of Ibn Battuta). Through this work, he created a sonic journey connecting different cultures across Africa, the Middle East, and Asia. Notable tracks from this release include:

Layali Beyrouth

Ifriquia

Raqset El Darbouka

Live Performances & Collaborations:

He frequently appears in high-profile concerts, music festivals, and television broadcasts across Morocco and the Arab world. Over his career, he has collaborated with major North African and Middle Eastern singers, orchestras, and fusion ensembles.

Social Media & Online Presence:

Under his Instagram handle (@antari_mustapha1), he regularly shares short solo performance clips, back-stage footage from studio sessions, live event highlights, and teaching/rhythmic masterclass snippets.
